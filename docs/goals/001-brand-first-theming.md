# 001. Brand-first theming

A project's look starts as a typed brand (subject, grounding, territories, muse, then closed choices), not as hand-set numbers. An agent with a brief and the guide arrives at the same brand a careful designer would, and the brand hydrates into a theme an app loads unchanged ([brand schema plan](../planning/brand-schema.md), [author a brand](../guides/author-a-brand.md)).

Met when every worked brief reproduces at [K-001](../kpi/001-brand-reproduction.md) ≥ 0.8 with no lint warnings, and a brand-built theme renders correctly in every engine (`apps/studio/brand-demo.html` in `check:browsers`).
