# 0012. White and black are neutral step 3; fg.strong

**Context.** #fff and #000 sat outside the palette, and headings had no strong color of their own.

**Decision.** white and black are neutral step 3 of the light and dark ramps (the same in every scheme; a theme maps either to another step or pure); fg.strong is black in light, white in dark, for display, headings and titles.

**Alternatives.** The step 1 endpoints; pure white and black.

**Consequences.** Strong text stays in the palette and follows a runtime spectrum.
