# Burger King brand expression references

## Sources and scope

- **Complete 129-page Brand Partners Guidelines**, with every physical page retained, including covers, separators, terms, contacts and the nearly blank closing page. Printed copyright is 2020. PDF metadata records creation on 20 January 2021 in Adobe InDesign16.0, 1920×1080pt, 7,470,869 bytes. This is the complete acquired **partner guide**, not a claim to possess Burger King’s unrestricted master identity archive.
- Original PDF: [burger-king-brand-guidelines-2020.pdf](../burger-king-brand-guidelines-2020.pdf). Retrieved 5 November 2026 from [Relayto’s public viewer](https://relayto.com/explore/burger-king-brand-book-ggvqmyg1vjfmm) through its published [download file](https://cdn.relayto.com/media/files/648766c497146b022873356c_burger-king-2020%20%281%29.pdf). Relayto is a third-party mirror; the book itself identifies Burger King Corporation. The viewer exposed all129 pages and its download. No Burger King-hosted original was verified.
- The proposed Evolve Branding PDF and a second Elbrus mirror returned404; PDFHost’s listed download returned502. These failed leads were not substituted with partial pages.
- **Complete distinct artwork sequence from [JKR’s Burger King case study](https://www.jkrglobal.com/work/burger-king)**: one hero animation plus13 body assets (9 stills and4 clips). The HTML snapshot and original WebP images are preserved in `sources/`. Five complete public Mux streams are remuxed without re-encoding into local MP4s. Original media URLs are recorded in [the media inventory](../sources/jkr-media-inventory.json). No separate still is created for a duplicate video poster or responsive derivative.
- JKR artwork supplements the PDF with spatial, packaging and moving applications; it does not replace book pages. An illustration can occur in the book and in a different case-study composition because the latter teaches a different application or sequence.

## Reading limits and source discrepancies

The guide carries internal-recipient, confidentiality, trademark and approval notices. They remain visible in the original pages and relevant manifests. Cataloging an example does not confer permission to publish its assets. The scope of every rule is historical to this edition; case-study observations are not new requirements.

- BK033 prints a page44 minimum-size cross-reference, but the minimum-size table appears on physical page45.
- BK053 refers to Product Architecture on page140, beyond this129-page partner file. That referenced material is unavailable here.
- BK062/063 retain the Fiery Red HEX/RGB mismatch rather than silently correcting it.
- BK071’s glyph-height hierarchy and BK085’s point-size wording are recorded separately. BK072’s reduced-leading example and BK073’s explicit75% rule are not conflated.
- BK085’s running heading and main heading differ. Several source cross-references are inaccurate; affected manifests record them.
- BK096’s no-cropping instruction coexists with cropped overview illustrations. The written instruction is preserved as a source rule; overview crops are not treated as permission.
- BK104’s prose calls the white stripe mayo, while its diagram labels it Onion Rings.
- Legal pages summarize the historical source, not current legal advice. Named contacts are preserved as source history, not verified current contacts.
- JKR clips were inspected at16 distributed timestamps each, including opening, middle and ending states. Manifest motion descriptions concern visible behavior; audio was not analyzed. Posters are selected source frames, not reconstructed boards.

## Verification and regeneration

All129 PDF pages were rendered at1920×1080 and visually inspected during decomposition. An independent Poppler render confirmed dimensions/page sequence and compared all129 images with PDFium output; maximum mean RGB difference was4.00/255, reflecting renderer/color differences. All nine case-study PNGs match decoded source pixels. Five complete clips are retained, approximately9.02,37.89,29.27,24.21 and12.10 seconds. Each manifest has evidence-backed purpose labels; nearly blank BK129 has an empty reviewed-purpose object.

From the parent directory, regenerate with `python3 build-gallery.py Burger-King "Burger King"`. The gallery embeds canonical JSON content and uses relative paths; no JSON fetch or server is needed.
