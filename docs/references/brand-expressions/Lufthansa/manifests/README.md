# Lufthansa — 2018 airline identity refresh

47 references: 45 still artworks and two animated comparisons. This collection preserves the complete accessible distinct still/GIF sequence from two designer case studies. It is not a complete brand book. It concerns the Lufthansa airline refresh of 2018, not the subsequent Lufthansa Group identity.

## Sources and coverage

- [Mona Franz — Lufthansa Brand Refresh](https://www.monafranz.de/project/lufthansa): 21 body image/GIF assets, in published order. The designer describes her work on the crane, type briefing, color/material and livery. Original HTML is preserved in [sources](../sources/mona-franz.html). Related-project footer thumbnails are outside this source scope.
- [HvD Fonts — Lufthansa](https://www.hvdfonts.com/custom-cases/lufthansa): 27 linked still/SVG artworks, including design process, type specimens and applications. The source names Hannes von Döhren, Martin et Karczinski and 2018. Its indexed page was readable through web retrieval, but direct HTTP and browser requests returned Cloudflare blocks; [the blocked response](../sources/hvd-fonts.html) is retained as access evidence, not as the original article. All 27 linked still/SVG originals were downloadable.
- Retrieved 2026-11-05. [Machine-readable inventory](../sources/inventory.json) retains original URLs, source families, dimensions and frame counts. File names and IDs follow this inventory. The source text of Mona Franz is preserved in its original HTML. HvD rationale in manifests is attributed and paraphrased; its entire article is not locally archived.

## Completeness and limits

- LH14 is reserved for the cropped Business Class check-in photograph in Mona Franz’s source. Its original JPG remains preserved; LH45 supplies the wider airport photograph, so no second cropped reference is cataloged. Material boards LH07/LH40 show different viewpoints and are retained. Aircraft renderings likewise have distinct views or context.
- Both sources embed the same two YouTube films: [Redesigning Lufthansa — Making of](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3h5hcHLnNUI) and [Lufthansa Brand Relaunch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hFlevJosql4). Retrieval failed with missing HTTPS formats/SABR and player reload errors. These are documented gaps, not analyzed motion references.
- HvD additionally exposes a native video placeholder in its article and a five-state type-construction explanation. The indexed page supplies one linked construction artwork, preserved as LH35. The inaccessible HTML prevents verifying/retrieving the native clip and four alternate interactive states. No complete motion or interactive coverage is claimed.
- No downloadable complete master guidelines were verified. Search results included editorial excerpts, case studies and references to Lufthansa Brand Management Online; these do not establish access to the master book.
- The original JPG/PNG rasters are retained at published resolution. PNG references preserve decoded source pixels; original transparency is retained for LH22/LH23. Five SVGs are rendered in Chromium at their viewBox dimensions, rounded upward for fractional heights, on a white background matching their specimen presentation. Originals remain available.
- LH06 and LH13 preserve their two-frame GIFs plus H.264 MP4 playback copies. Every frame was inspected. Their PNGs are first-frame posters. These are comparative specimen animations, not evidence of general brand motion rules.
- Small labels on photographed process boards are not treated as authoritative production specifications. Observed examples do not establish mandatory ratios, sizes or clearspace.
