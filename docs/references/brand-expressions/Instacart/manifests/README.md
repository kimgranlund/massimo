# Instacart · Brand Guidelines v1.0

[Open the offline gallery](../index.html). The February 25, 2022 deck introduces the Shop + Savor framework and follows it through identity elements, layout, motion and applications. Every physical page of the 100-page source file appears here, including section openers, example-only boards and the contact page. The edition records work in progress: page 33 marks the typeface section as unfinished, and page 89 introduces application examples with a qualification.

The complete local PDF is a copy hosted by Bárbara Josa. Its cover identifies the brand, edition and date. Wolff Olins documents the collaboration with Instacart and the design strategy behind the carrot and wordmark. Instacart’s press hub links to a newer online brand guide. This collection records the dated PDF edition. [Source PDF](https://barbarajosa.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/instacart-2022.pdf) · [Local PDF](../instacart-brand-guidelines-v1-2022.pdf) · [Wolff Olins project](https://www.wolffolins.com/work/instacart) · [Instacart press hub](https://company.instacart.com/press) · [Instacart brand guide](https://heyitsinstacart.com/)
