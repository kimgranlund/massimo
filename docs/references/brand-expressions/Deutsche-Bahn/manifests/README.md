# Deutsche Bahn — new design guidelines

This collection preserves the complete public **Neues Design** section set captured on **2026-11-05**: 15 official website pages, all 160 direct content sections, 12 additional tab/slider states, 48 embedded video demonstrations and 10 GIF demonstrations. The motion header film is attached to its existing section reference. Total: **230 PNG/manifest pairs and 59 playable clips**.

These are clearly identified **webpage section captures and original motion demonstrations**, not slides from an authored brand book. No complete PDF was exposed on these pages. The edition is undated. The source's “end of September” toolkit notice does not identify a year or establish that a toolkit download was available.

## Source boundary

The starting legacy Design page links to a distinct new-design site. This collection covers that new site's entire public navigation: overview, three office application pages, personality, principles and all nine ingredient/application chapters. It does not mix in the legacy Puls rules, which differ from the new Schwelle system. Legal/contact pages and external office-software administration are outside the design-reference scope. Original linked asset files are retained; this is not a functional reimplementation of DB's Teams or signature generators.

## Preservation and capture method

- All 468 directly linked resource assets retrieved successfully and retained with exact URLs in [asset-inventory.json](../sources/asset-inventory.json). Original response HTML and extracted source text remain alongside the full-page screenshots.
- Browser viewport: 1440 × 1000 at device scale 1. The cookie-analysis prompt was declined. Sticky portal navigation was hidden for capture so it could not obscure content. Brand content, typography, layout and colors were not restyled. All native disclosure panels were expanded. Native tabs and comparison sliders were captured at every additional relevant state.
- PNGs are literal crops of preserved full-page screenshots. [sections.json](../sources/sections.json) gives `source_capture` and exact `rect` coordinates for every one of the 172 section/state images. All 172 passed exact RGB pixel comparison against those source crops. The source screenshots, rather than the remote site, are each manifest's offline source link.
- Embedded video consent panels remain visible in section screenshots: the screenshots preserve that observed source state rather than substitute fabricated player artwork. All 48 corresponding public films were independently acquired from their original HLS configurations, remuxed without visual re-encoding, and supplied as separate playable references. [video-inventory.json](../sources/video-inventory.json) maps captions, sections, original configuration URLs, local files and durations. There are no unavailable embedded films in this bounded source set.
- The ten original GIFs are preserved. Their MP4 playback copies use H.264/yuv420p at 100 fps so centisecond GIF timing can be represented without variable-frame-rate browser playback problems. [extra-motion.json](../sources/extra-motion.json) records source URLs and local media paths. The native header MP4 is preserved separately and plays on DB1500.
- Every image was visually inspected. Every film and GIF was inspected at eight positions across its complete duration; text/curve numbers were checked against source text. Motion posters use native-frame resolution. Section screenshots are still states, not proof of temporal behavior. Analysis covers visual behavior; no sonic character is inferred from a silent frame or from the source’s mention of a sound logo.
- Responsive duplicate image nodes are represented once in each rendered section. Repeated ingredients remain when their surrounding section teaches a different rule. Layout 01 and the DOOH example retain separate published films and contextual roles; the positive comparison row reuses a composition to teach sequencing against its negative partner. Source bytes remain preserved even when related artwork recurs.

## Source qualifications

- The new site supplies some inconsistent local rules. DB1009 says Signature Graphics replace the Schwelle; DB1204 allows coexistence with separation; DB1212 limits coexistence to a subtle background Schwelle. The relevant manifests preserve these local statements rather than silently invent one governing rule.
- Red text guidance has different local scopes: DB0811 allows limited red UI text while DB0813 generally rejects red type. Typography naming varies between “Head Extra Wide” and “Wide Extra Black”; source terms are retained where used.
- Several motion captions mismatch their linked films. The headline examples DBV15/16 reverse the printed centered/left labels. Body-text and expressive-type captions and some transition labels also conflict with the visible films. Manifests describe the actual media and separately identify source-caption issues. No timing or direction is inferred from a mislabeled filename alone.
- DB1307 comparison controls intentionally expose gray gradient/overlay artwork on one side; that gray is part of the original source asset, not a failed download. Both full endpoint states are captured in DB1307B/C. DB1409B/C similarly preserve both photography-processing states.
- Social-safe diagrams are preserved in DB1506B rather than extrapolating the classic-video 5% Action Safe / 10% Title Safe margins to all platforms.
- Strategic value fields are interpretations phrased as possible benefits. Intent purpose tags require explicit source explanation; Constraint tags preserve actual limits, exceptions and medium-specific scope.
