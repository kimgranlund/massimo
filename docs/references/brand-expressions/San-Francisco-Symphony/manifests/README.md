# San Francisco Symphony — COLLINS case study

Source: https://wearecollins.com/case-studies/san-francisco-symphony/ (retrieved 2026-11-05).

## Scope and provenance

34 distinct references cover the complete published case-study artwork sequence: 21 still artworks, 12 Mux clips and one animated GIF. The source cover (SF01) repeats the facade photograph in SF09 at lower resolution; its original is retained but no duplicate manifest is created. Annotated augments SF03–SF05 remain distinct from unannotated examples because their commentary adds teaching. Press thumbnails and unrelated recommended projects are excluded. No case-study-body media was unavailable. This is a case study, not a complete brand manual.

The work depicts the historical identity around the Salonen transition, including 2021 program examples and 2022 monthly covers. The current agency page adds explanatory augment material; it is not represented as a dated original guideline deck. Agency business-outcome claims are preserved in source JSON but not treated as verified causal results in these manifests.

Original full-resolution JPEGs and GIF are retained. Mux videos are losslessly remuxed from the highest available public HLS rendition to local MP4; source playlist URLs are in [asset-inventory.json](../sources/asset-inventory.json). SF34 also has an H.264 playback derivative of its original ten-frame, 15-second GIF. PNG stills preserve source pixels; video PNGs are representative frames, not substitutes for the full clips. Every still and temporal samples across every clip were inspected. Audio tracks are retained when present but sound-specific claims are not made from unheard audio. The source explains sound-responsive behavior; that rationale is attributed to COLLINS.
