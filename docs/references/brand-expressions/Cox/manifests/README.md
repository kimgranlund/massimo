# Cox retail expression references

This collection contains all 19 distinct visible image assets from [Lindsay Little’s Cox Brand Collection](https://www.lindsay-little.com/cox-2017), credited to Periscope with creative direction and art direction by Lindsay Little. The portfolio describes work developed over three years. Its URL uses 2017, while several source filenames refer to 2018. Treat this as a historical portfolio collection, not a single dated corporate guideline edition.

## Scope and source limits

- CR01–CR19 preserve the source page’s visual order. Responsive resolutions and repeated modal thumbnails are deduplicated. Original composite boards remain intact because their comparisons are meaningful.
- Fifteen entries are still artworks or photographs. CR02, CR10, CR15 and CR19 are the four supplied Vimeo posters. They are explicitly labelled as posters and do not claim to document motion. Public player retrieval returned authorization errors, so no video files are included.
- Video references are [brand awareness / connection](https://vimeo.com/383153415), [virtual home tour](https://vimeo.com/382481046), [brand awareness / streaming](https://vimeo.com/383158482), and [cables, cords and power packs](https://vimeo.com/382481008). These external provenance links require a network. The gallery and all its assets work offline.
- The separately requested [Brady Boyle identity case study](https://www.bradypatrickboyle.com/cox-communications) redirects to `https://slabside.design/cox-communications`, which returned HTTP 404 on 2026-11-05. Both www and non-www source variants were checked. Search results retain descriptive text, but the original visual assets could not be retrieved. That identity series is not included or reconstructed.
- The downloaded originals are retained under `sources/`. [The source inventory](../sources/inventory.json) records each original asset URL, filename and pixel dimensions. PNG pairs retain the entire source at the best resolution exposed by the page, up to 2500 pixels wide. No contact-sheet crops or editorial replacement slides are used.
- These manifests interpret visible choices. Ingredient records explicitly distinguish observed examples from general requirements. A photo does not establish a measured audience response or a company-wide rule.

## Gallery maintenance

From the parent directory, run `python3 build-gallery.py Cox "Cox" "Retail expression collection · 19 references, including four video posters. The separate identity case study is currently unavailable."` after changing a manifest. The HTML embeds its specifications and uses relative local links. Open [the gallery](../index.html) directly from disk.
