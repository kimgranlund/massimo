- 15 body-media entries: six original JPEG artworks and nine complete motion files. Every body entry is included in source order. No missing body media.
- Original published MOV production files are preserved alongside H.264/AAC MP4 playback copies. Full durations are retained. Original still dimensions are preserved as lossless PNG conversions.
- AU04 and AU05 reorganize the same eight principles into different portrait and landscape arrangements. Both are retained because the reflow is a distinct layout lesson, not merely a resized duplicate.
- AU02 and AU12 are separately authored autoplay edits for the source page. They share footage with AU01 and AU11 respectively but have different sequences and durations. Both introductory edits and complete films are retained and labeled.
- The two additional source metadata images (`kms-team-audi_OG.jpg`, `kms-team-audi_T.jpg`) are preserved but not cataloged: they are social/teaser metadata, not additional body artworks. Responsive JPEG/WebP renditions of the same still are not separately cataloged; the original URL version is used.
- Video PNGs are single frames extracted from the original MOVs; their exact timestamps are recorded in each manifest. They are not composite boards. The accompanying MP4 is required to inspect the full sequence.
- AU04's original is 768×1385 and AU05's original is 1680×945. Their playback copies have one black row appended at the bottom for even-dimension H.264 compatibility. PNG posters retain original dimensions; no source pixels are cropped.
- Each still and every clip were visually inspected. Twelve evenly distributed frames were reviewed for every clip; the 89.48-second overview was additionally inspected at three-second intervals. Manifest descriptions concern visible behavior; audio content was not analyzed.
