# Slack

[Open the offline gallery](../index.html). September 2020 edition covering brand voice, design elements, illustration and video treatments.

## Source and coverage

All 50 physical pages of the [local original](../slack-brand-guidelines-september-2020.pdf) have a full-page PNG and a six-field manifest in PDF order, including any covers, dividers and blank leaves. Wordless illustrations receive descriptive catalog titles. Video treatment pages reproduce the stills in the book.
