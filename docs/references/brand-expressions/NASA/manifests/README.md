# NASA Graphics Standards Manual

[Open the offline gallery](../index.html). January 1976 manual, including logotype, typography, publications, signage and motor vehicles.

## Source and coverage

All 60 physical pages of the [local original](../nasa-graphics-standards-manual-1976.pdf) have a full-page PNG and a six-field manifest in PDF order, including any covers, dividers and blank leaves. The original is a scan of NHB 1430.2. PDF locations are one-based; the manual also uses section folios. IDs V01, V02 and V03 remain associated with physical pages 48, 50 and 52.

[Original PDF from NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/nasa_graphics_manual_nhb_1430-2_jan_1976.pdf)
