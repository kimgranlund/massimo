# NASA vehicle identification

[Open the offline gallery](../index.html).

Three selected pages from the National Aeronautics and Space Administration’s *Graphics Standards Manual*, NHB 1430.2, January 1976. The official PDF is a later scan of the manual. This collection follows three decisions through section 7: choose a foreground against the paint, fit the identification to a door, then adapt it across a fleet. The complete source is preserved alongside the collection. The PDF contains 60 pages. Each PNG renders its complete source page at 150 dpi, including the scan margins and binder holes. The PDF page numbers count from one. Manual section 7.1 (PDF page 47) contains the ten-step gray scale referenced by V01, and section 7.3 (PDF page 49) explains the red-logo option for white vehicles. Section 7.5 (PDF page 51) describes a door configuration with its logotype placed elsewhere. The selected pages are a focused historical reference. The numbers and treatments describe the January 1976 system.
