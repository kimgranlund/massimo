# MIT CSAIL

[Open the offline gallery](../index.html). Our Branding: The Short Guide traces the lab’s identity from architecture to logos, patterns, typography and applications.

## Source and coverage

All 38 physical pages of the [local original](../csail-branding-short-guide.pdf) have a full-page PNG and a six-field manifest in PDF order, including any covers, dividers and blank leaves. The original comes from MIT’s Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory. PDF metadata records July 2020. Physical page 2 is a blank leaf and retains its place.
