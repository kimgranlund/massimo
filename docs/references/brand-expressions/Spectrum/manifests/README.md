# Spectrum reference inventory

[Open the offline gallery](../index.html).

## Sources and scope

- **S01–S31** preserve every physical page of the August 2017 Spectrum Retail Mall Guide, including covers, dividers and operational pages. [Local original PDF](../spectrum-retail-mall-guide-2017.pdf). [Public portfolio-hosted PDF](https://media.journoportfolio.com/users/376804/uploads/0b3e08a7-70eb-468e-b22f-c637f68122f6.pdf). The publisher is Spectrum/Charter as identified in the document. The public host is a portfolio CDN, not Charter’s own site. The guide is historical; contacts, products, claims and links are not verified as current.
- **E01, E03–E09** preserve the hero artwork and all seven directly published MP4 visualizations from [Thornberg & Forester’s Spectrum Brand Evolution case study](https://thornbergandforester.com/project/charter-communications-spectrum-brand-evolution/), published January 2025 according to page metadata. These are original agency presentations, not a complete brand manual or production toolkit. The videos remain local and playable offline. Each PNG is an unaltered single frame from its matching clip; its time and the clip duration are recorded in the manifest.
- **E02 is reserved, not ingested.** The page’s separate Vimeo overview iframe (video 1044004898) returned HTTP 401/403 during retrieval. No poster or substitute is presented as the missing clip. This limit does not affect the seven direct MP4s.
- The page’s 14-pixel decorative spacer and website navigation are excluded from the artwork inventory. E09 is a montage of exploratory concepts and is explicitly distinguished from the selected final identity.
- Direct media URLs are recorded in [asset-origins.json](../sources/asset-origins.json). [media-metadata.json](../sources/media-metadata.json) records clip durations and poster timestamps. The signed original video URLs may expire; the local originals remain available.

## Interpretation boundaries

The 2017 guide specifies a two-blue retail system. The later case study describes retaining a blue presence while adding vivid green, adapting the connectivity current to screen formats, and slowing motion for large architectural displays. These sources document different periods and scopes. Their rules must not be combined into one undated specification.

The agency explains the selected current as a visual connection to Life Unlimited. Inferred brand benefits in the manifests use “can.” Palette values are transcribed only where the PDF provides them. The historical contrast examples are not treated as measured accessibility certification. The broad expanded palette is documented for the lobby installation, not generalized to all Spectrum materials.

All 31 PDF pages were visually inspected as rendered pages. Every case-study clip was inspected at multiple timestamps covering its duration. Files preserve source proportions and artwork. The source PDF renders are 1584 × 1224 pixels.

## Gallery maintenance

From the parent directory, run `python3 build-gallery.py Spectrum "Spectrum" "Complete August 2017 retail guide · 31 pages. Life Unlimited evolution · 8 case-study visuals, including 7 local motion clips."` after editing manifests. All gallery assets and links are relative, and no server or JSON fetch is required.
