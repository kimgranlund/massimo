# Natural History Museum — Pentagram and Nomad case study

[Open offline gallery](../index.html)

## Source and coverage

Source: [Pentagram / Natural History Museum](https://www.pentagram.com/work/natural-history-museum). Retrieved 2026-11-05. This is the complete distinct project-body visual sequence exposed by that page: hero plus 24 media entries, yielding **25 references: 15 still artworks and 10 complete motion presentations**. It is an agency case study, not a complete guidelines book. The source includes a 2023 season cover; its page does not provide an explicit edition date. Preserve its historical project scope rather than treating examples as current mandatory standards.

The sequence and captions are preserved in [original page HTML](../sources/pentagram.html), [extracted source text](../sources/source-text.txt), and [media inventory](../sources/inventory.json). The inventory records exact signed published still URLs, Vimeo IDs, dimensions, duration and local stream information. Published lightbox stills are up to 2000px wide; video covers are 1920px and the hero poster is 1717px. Responsive derivatives of the same artwork are not separate references. The hero film and full case film are retained separately because they demonstrate different sequences. Video cover PNGs remain the publisher’s original poster, not invented representative boards.

All ten public Vimeo HLS presentations were retrieved and remuxed to local MP4 with stream copy, retaining video/audio streams. No missing project media remains. These are published web renditions, not claimed camera or design masters. Each still and poster was visually inspected. Twelve frames spaced across each complete clip were inspected; full streams were also decoded to check integrity. Audio is preserved but not independently analyzed; source statements about sound-reactivity are explicitly attributed. No audio timing or sound-design rules are inferred.

## Reading and limits

Intent and strategic value are analyst interpretations unless reference-purpose evidence explicitly attributes a rationale to the source. No mandatory color proportions, spacing limits or motion timing are inferred from applications. Tiny generator controls and fine print are not transcribed. Natural specimens are described at a safe level where identity is uncertain. Numerical conservation statements visible in campaign examples are treated as source copy, not independently verified present-day facts.
