# Instagram brand design system references

[Open the offline gallery](../index.html).

## Source and scope

This collection preserves all 34 distinct visual assets in the main body of [Elvis Dinh’s Instagram brand design system case study](https://www.elvisdinh.com/instagram-brand-design-system). The page identifies the project year as **2022** and credits Dinh as lead brand designer for systems. It credits collaboration with 2x4, Studio Dumbar, Oddfellows and Johannes Leonardo. It is a designer’s retrospective case study, not a complete downloadable master brand book. Later asset upload filenames on the live page do not establish new guideline editions.

The source explains a system that can reduce or increase its presence. Its surrounding text identifies three principles—connection, expression and imperfect human experience—and distinguishes quiet informational or partnership work, core consumer marketing and more expressive cultural moments. Manifests attribute this accompanying rationale explicitly when the artwork alone does not state it. No measured audience effects are inferred from the examples.

## Inventory and media

- IG01–IG34 preserve main-page visual order. Related-project thumbnails, navigation and site decoration are excluded. Responsive image variants are deduplicated. The different views of the outdoor installation and football activation remain separate because they show different scales and applications.
- All 25 still images and seven animated GIFs were downloaded at the original resolutions exposed by the page. Originals remain in `sources/`. The [asset inventory](../sources/inventory.json) records original URLs, dimensions, frame counts and poster selections.
- IG02–IG05, IG17, IG24 and IG25 retain their original GIFs and have local H.264 MP4 playback copies. The PNG for each is an unaltered selected frame. The manifest records its source frame, timestamp and original sequence duration. Conversion preserves the complete sequence and pads an odd frame dimension only where required by the video codec. It adds no designed content or audio.
- IG01 and IG23 are the original supplied Vimeo posters. The [brand overview](https://vimeo.com/885032652) and [motion sizzle](https://vimeo.com/889948312) returned HTTP 403 from their player configuration endpoints during retrieval. These entries are explicitly poster-only. No unavailable motion behavior is described from those stills.
- Seven GIFs were inspected at five points across their duration. The glyph and layout diagrams include instructional measurements; these are scoped to their demonstrated constructions. The other campaign and event images are observed examples, not universal application rules.
- Some artwork is only available at small source sizes, including the 494 × 880 campaign examples. PNG conversion preserves those native dimensions rather than inventing detail.

## Gallery maintenance

From the parent directory, run `python3 build-gallery.py Instagram "Instagram" "2022 project retrospective · 34 references, including 7 local animation clips and 2 video posters. Case study, not a complete master brand book."` after editing a manifest. The gallery embeds its specs, uses relative links and works directly from disk.
