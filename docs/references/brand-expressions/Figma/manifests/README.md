# Figma — Nothing Great Is Made Alone

Source: [COLLINS case study](https://wearecollins.com/case-studies/figma/), retrieved 2026-11-05. This is the complete distinct artwork/media sequence available in the case-study payload, not a master brand guideline book. The source page gives no campaign edition date; do not treat these examples as current product specifications.

## Coverage and provenance

- 24 references: one separately published cover film, four annotated Augments boards and all 19 gallery items. The cover still is identical to gallery item 14 (FG19) and is represented once. The separate cover film shares logo-reveal footage with FG06 but is retained as its own independently presented identity sequence.
- All 15 still originals and nine complete MP4 renditions were retrieved. No media gaps were found in this case-study body. Related case studies, navigation and website furniture are excluded.
- Native published still dimensions are retained, including four 12500 × 8333 boards. PNGs are full RGB conversions without crop or rescaling. Every still was visually inspected. Motion was inspected at eight distributed points across each full clip; the complete clips remain playable locally. Audio is preserved where supplied but was not analyzed; manifests make no sonic claims.
- PNG video posters come from the midpoint of each clip and are not presented as complete motion evidence. Original Mux high.mp4 renditions are retained unmodified.
- The agency’s story and Augments descriptions are preserved in [decoded source data](../sources/figma-decoded.json); original [HTML](../sources/figma.html) and [payload](../sources/figma-payload.json) are also retained. [Source inventory](../sources/inventory.json) records every URL and clip probe.
- Orange handwriting on FG02–FG04 belongs to the source boards. These are not annotations added during ingestion. FG03 includes categories in handwriting that differ from the visible order; the manifest describes the actual groupings.
- FG21–FG23 show the same installation at distinct viewpoints and scales: room-scale composition, depth, and material detail. They are retained because each teaches a different application decision.
- Source illustrations and historical captions are campaign storytelling, not independently verified art or science history. No source board prescribes clear-space, palette percentages, timings or universal composition rules.
