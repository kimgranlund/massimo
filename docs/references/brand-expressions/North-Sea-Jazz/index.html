NN North Sea Jazz Festival

Three applications from Studio Dumbar’s project. Play the lineup to see how the weekday moves while the schedule holds long enough to read.

NS01  /  Typography

Red transit poster

Slide intent
Teach designers to suggest movement in a still piece of lettering. Changes in spacing and direction can lead the eye through a static word.
Strategic value
The festival can give a printed poster the same rhythmic character as its moving announcements. The small white festival signature anchors the more freely shaped headline.
Demonstration
On a black poster, the red words NORTH, SEA and JAZZ stretch into bands of horizontal lines. The letter groups change angle as they descend. A narrow footer carries small white marks and the festival name.
Category
Typography
Practical value
Separate the expressive type from the identifying information. A viewer can encounter the deformed headline at street scale and still find the stable festival signature below.
Content islands
  1. Street setting Keep the entire photograph. The narrow poster case stands near the center, beside a pole and in front of a passing transit vehicle. The surroundings establish viewing scale.
  2. Festival name Fill the upper poster with red capitals on black. Stack NORTH, SEA and JAZZ so the widths and angles change from line to line. Interrupt portions of the letterforms with parallel horizontal gaps.
  3. Identity footer Reserve a short band at the foot for marks and small text. Place the compact white North Sea Jazz signature at lower right, clear of the patterned headline.

NS02  /  Motion

Three-day animated lineup

Slide intent
Teach motion designers to give information a rhythm and a reading interval. A reveal can invite attention, and a held arrangement gives someone time to read the lineup.
Strategic value
The festival can use a recurring motion phrase to connect its three days. Changing the hue and weekday identifies the next lineup while the shared reveal ties the posts together.
Demonstration
A portrait post cycles through cyan Friday, red Saturday and pink Sunday. Thin slices gather into a large weekday and compact performer rows. The settled lists hold before they fragment for the next day.
Category
Motion
Practical value
Give the moving headline and the schedule different duties. At 0.8 seconds Friday is still emerging. Around 2.4 seconds its list has settled into ruled rows. Compare those moments when choosing when the audience should read.
Content islands
  1. Post frame Center the vertical post within the black field. Keep the muted gray interface strips above and below the colored content while the schedule changes.
  2. Weekday reveal Fill the upper part of the post with one bold uppercase weekday. Begin the Friday name in separated cyan bands, then bring the fragments together until the word reads solidly.
  3. Lineup Reveal compact performer names beneath the weekday in the same hue. Use thin dividing rules and hold the aligned list before the transition resumes.
  4. Day transitions Repeat the phrase in red for Saturday and pink for Sunday. Preserve the full clip so viewers can inspect the transitions and the holds alongside the selected still.

NS03  /  Application

Friday festival program cover

Slide intent
Show how a publication can tell someone which program to pick up at a glance. Giving the day and date most of the cover lets edition information carry the composition.
Strategic value
The festival can connect a practical timetable to its larger identity. The cyan date and striped lettering recall the campaign, while the small signature identifies the organizer.
Demonstration
A hand holds a black program at an angle. Large cyan lettering gives Friday, 8 July and 2022 the dominant area. Thin parallel cuts interrupt the strokes. White marks and a FESTIVAL PROGRAM label sit below.
Category
Application
Practical value
Use this cover when deciding which words to enlarge on a daily program. The edition date leads, and the publication label remains readable in a separate corner.
Content islands
  1. Hand-held publication Retain the photographic frame and diagonal booklet. The hand establishes scale and partially overlaps the artwork.
  2. Date field Stack the weekday, day and month, and year across the black cover in large cyan capitals and numerals. Let the thin cuts in the letterforms echo the animated campaign.
  3. Publication identifiers Place the compact white festival signature in the lower left of the cover and the white two-line publication label at lower right. Leave a black band between these identifiers and the date.
