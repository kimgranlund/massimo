# MIT Media Lab — historical Pentagram identity

[Open the offline gallery](../index.html). 29 reference units: all 28 distinct still artworks in the project gallery and the complete embedded film. This is a case-study series, not a downloaded guidelines book. The collection is distinct from MIT CSAIL.

## Provenance and scope

- [Primary case study](https://www.pentagram.com/work/mit-media-lab), Pentagram, Michael Bierut; client Massachusetts Institute of Technology.
- [Pentagram archive record 20643](https://www.pentagram.com/archive/item/20643), historical 2014 identity. No claim that these are current operating standards.
- [Original film](https://vimeo.com/109891648), Pentagram; retained as the complete public 720p MP4, about 58 seconds. Film appears before stills on the source page; it is indexed ML29 to preserve a contiguous still-image sequence.
- Retrieved 2026-11-05. Full signed image URLs, source filenames, sizes and SHA-256 hashes: [inventory](../sources/inventory.json).

The source narrative links a common seven-by-seven construction grid to the earlier anniversary identity and describes extending the approach to 23 research groups. It explains the balance between institutional continuity and group variety, and connects Helvetica with the institution’s communication history. Analyst interpretations in the manifests are distinguished from this stated rationale by the purpose evidence. No artwork provides a prohibited-use or measurable limit; no Constraint tags are assigned.

## Preservation and limits

The 28 stills use the published 2000px lightbox renditions, preserving their complete published composition. Unsigned raw image URLs returned HTTP 403. Responsive versions and repeated thumbnails are omitted; ML02 retains the crop explicitly published by the source. No unlinked numbered file is guessed into the series. The film’s source poster is not counted as another artwork. Related-project and news images are excluded. PNGs are lossless conversions of the downloaded JPEG rendition (decoded pixels compared equal); they do not imply access to uncropped camera originals. No replacement board was drawn.

All stills were visually inspected. The entire film was sampled at three-second intervals, spanning opening, group sequences, collective arrays and ending; the full clip remains available offline for continuous viewing. ML29 PNG is the 45-second poster frame. Application descriptions are observations, not comprehensive specifications of printing, accessibility, clear space, material finishes, font metrics or motion timing.
