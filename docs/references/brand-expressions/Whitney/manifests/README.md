# Whitney / Responsive W

28 references from the complete distinct artwork sequence in Experimental Jetset’s [2013 process essay](https://www.jetset.nl/archive/whitney-museum-identity) and the directly linked [official museum launch page](https://whitney.org/about/new-identity). Retrieved 2026-11-05. This is a process-and-application collection, **not the complete 220-page Whitney Graphic Manual**.

## Scope and provenance

- WH01–WH17 retain all 17 studio-page artworks in source order: 16 stills and the published poster for the complete introduction film. Enlargements replace their corresponding thumbnails. The abstract chart is linked as a hidden lightbox image and is included.
- WH18–WH28 retain all 11 official launch-page application photographs in source order, credited there to Jens Mortensen; graphic materials are credited to the museum’s Graphic Design department. These are distinct from the studio’s preliminary proposals.
- The studio describes the 220-page manual but provides no PDF download. Its photographed cover is WH02; this does not establish access to the interior pages. Preliminary sketches and signage proposals remain identified as proposals, not installed or current standards.
- Original HTML is retained as `sources/source.html` and `sources/whitney-launch.html`. These are provenance snapshots, not offline galleries. Original source URLs and filenames are also in `sources/inventory.json`.
- WH05 uses the accessible 523 × 667 source image corresponding to the page’s 250 × 319 thumbnail. Other linked enlargements and the launch page’s largest published 2048px images are retained. Some unlinked studio sketches are intrinsically small; no missing detail is invented or upscaled into apparent evidence.
- WH12’s small blue annotations are not fully legible at the published resolution. Its manifest records visible construction/placement groups and explicit adjacent prose without guessing measurement values.
- The current official launch page gives WH25 an unrelated caption and alt text about a mobile gallery/RV. The actual image and filename show membership materials. The manifest relies on visible evidence and records this metadata mismatch.
- WH09 is Ad Reinhardt’s 1946 cartoon, presented by the studio as a conceptual reference. It is not a Whitney guideline.
- The full 32.62-second introduction MOV is preserved. A local MP4 remux keeps the original H.264 video and AAC audio without re-encoding. Twelve frames from 0–32 seconds were inspected across its duration; audio is retained but not analyzed. The PNG is the original published video poster, including its play symbol.
- No case-study artwork is omitted. Duplicate thumbnails are represented by their larger counterpart; site navigation, decorative logos and the unrelated site-wide “On the Hour” art overlay are outside the identity source body.
