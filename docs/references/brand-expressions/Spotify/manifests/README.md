# Spotify — COLLINS case study

[Open offline gallery](../index.html)

Scope: the complete accessible distinct artwork sequence of the [COLLINS Spotify case study](https://wearecollins.com/case-studies/spotify/), retrieved 2026-11-05. This is an agency retrospective of a historical identity project, with later product examples; it is not a current official brand book or a single dated guideline edition. The source does not date each artwork. Titles without source captions are descriptive labels.

22 PNG/JSON pairs: 18 still compositions, three animated demonstrations and one 51.06-second product film. Original image assets, all three original GIFs, MP4 playback derivatives and the downloaded film are local. Native still dimensions are preserved; animation posters are single source frames, not fabricated composite slides.

## Reconciliation and limits

- Source order: cover; four named mechanism illustrations; 17 gallery images; three empty gallery image records; one Running film.
- SP09 is intentionally unassigned: the fourth gallery image repeats the cover composition at lower resolution. SP01 retains the 2880 × 3600 original; sources/sp09.png preserves the repeated 1920 × 2400 asset.
- SP05 and SP15 share a stage photograph but remain distinct: SP05 contains explanatory annotations and margins; SP15 is the unannotated application photograph.
- Three image records immediately before the film have no asset reference, URL, dimensions or artwork in the source payload. They cannot be recovered from this source and have no invented manifest.
- Article/press thumbnails, navigation, related case studies and site UI are excluded from the artwork sequence.
- Small overview-board numeric specifications and several handwritten annotations cannot be transcribed reliably. No exact rules are inferred from them. No Constraint purpose is assigned.
- Animations were sampled at eight points across each duration: Lens 10.8 seconds / 18 frames; Vibrating Color 12 seconds / 24 frames; Memory Marker 7.2 seconds / 60 frames. The film was sampled every five seconds across its 51.06-second duration. Local playback retains full sequences and film audio.
- Artist identities are transcribed only where visible in artwork. Source narrative benefits are distinguished from inferred strategic values.
- sources/case-study-evidence.json preserves the decoded case-study content and asset metadata, excluding unrelated case studies and embedded low-quality previews.

From the parent brand-expressions folder run:

```sh
python3 build-gallery.py Spotify "Spotify" "COLLINS identity retrospective: 22 distinct artworks and motion sequences. Historical case study; not a current complete brand guide."
```

The generated gallery embeds its specs and uses relative local links; no server or network is needed.
