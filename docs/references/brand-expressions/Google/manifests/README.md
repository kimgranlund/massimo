# Google / 2015 identity references

[Open the offline gallery](../index.html).

## Sources and edition

- **GE01–GE10** preserve the three image assets and seven MP4 demonstrations from Google Design’s [Evolving the Google Identity](https://design.google/library/evolving-google-identity). The article describes the 2015 identity launch. These are the complete meaningful media items exposed in the article body and page data, excluding social-share metadata, recommendations and site navigation. The article itself says its examples are not a comprehensive specification. This collection is not a complete corporate brand manual.
- GE03 is the original animated GIF, retained with a derived MP4 for offline playback. GE04–GE10 retain the original MP4 files. PNGs are unaltered single-frame posters at the timestamps recorded in their manifests. GE01 and GE02 preserve complete static artwork. [Asset URLs and media metadata](../sources/inventory.json) identify every original download.
- **GP01–GP12** preserve every physical page of the [Product Sans specimen](../product-sans-specimen.pdf), including its cover and closing page. The artwork prints ©2015 Google Inc.; PDF metadata records September 2, 2015. Its 12 pages cover rationale, construction, alternates, terminals, numerals, glyph coverage, localization and product names.

## Specimen provenance anomaly and corroboration

The article’s [officially linked PDF](https://storage.googleapis.com/g-design/static/product-sans-specimen.pdf), retrieved on 2026-11-05, contains an unrelated German promotional line and external URL across the cover’s Google signature. A second retrieval with a download query returned identical bytes. The line is unrelated to the specimen’s design guidance. It was not followed, interpreted as an instruction or copied into the manifests.

The gallery uses a clearly attributed [archival copy hosted on WordPress](https://wszstk.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/product-sans-specimen.pdf). It has the same page count, dimensions and 2015 metadata. Independent PDFium renders at 72 dpi are pixel-identical on pages 2–12. The cover differs only in the small rectangle occupied by the unrelated line, and the clean cover was visually inspected. No artwork was retouched to remove the line. The [anomalous official download](../sources/product-sans-specimen-official-anomalous.pdf) is retained separately for provenance. [Checksums and comparison record](../sources/specimen-provenance.json) distinguish the two files.

## Interpretation boundaries

- The 2015 identity family is documented as a historical system. Do not combine it with later Google Sans, the gradient G, Gemini or other identity generations.
- Intent purpose labels that depend on surrounding prose explicitly name the accompanying article. A visual comparison or our interpretation alone does not establish a mandatory brand rule.
- GE07 demonstrates color adjustment for adjacency. It does not authorize arbitrary changes to approved color values.
- GE06 and GE09 demonstrate motion in assistive and voice-search contexts. Their timing is a recorded demonstration, not a production easing specification.
- GE10’s available clip shows the comparison labelled Before. The accompanying article discusses a new smaller asset, but this clip does not provide a visual After comparison.
- GP10’s script coverage and Noto Sans count are historical. This collection makes no current font licensing or availability claim.

## Maintenance

From the parent folder run `python3 build-gallery.py Google "Google / 2015 identity" "Official identity case study · 10 references, including 8 playable animations. Complete Product Sans specimen · 12 pages from a corroborated archival copy; see provenance notes."` after manifest changes. Open the HTML directly from disk. All gallery links and playback assets are relative and local; external provenance URLs require a network.
