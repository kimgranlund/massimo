## Instagram

The library now includes the accessible visual series from Elvis Dinh’s 2022 identity case study, with animation and poster-only limits recorded in its inventory. A complete master identity book remains on the wishlist. [Offline Instagram gallery](Instagram/index.html) · [Source inventory](Instagram/manifests/README.md)

Lead designer Elvis Dinh shows how the 2022 system ranges from quiet to expressive applications. Meta publishes brand resources and an account of its later refresh. These sources identify the system and its authors; the public pages examined did not supply a complete downloadable master identity deck. [Designer’s project](https://www.elvisdinh.com/instagram-brand-design-system) · [Meta’s identity article](https://www.meta.com/design-at-meta/blog/the-new-instagram-brand-identity/) · [Instagram brand resources](https://www.meta.com/en-gb/brand/resources/instagram/instagram-brand/)

Two third-party listings describe a ten-page historical Instagram usage guide covering icons, screenshots and permissions. Its original publisher download and complete page artwork could not be verified. ## Nike Sportswear

The library now includes Annie Nguyen’s complete accessible Holiday 2019 case-study sequence: nine Sportswear artworks and eight separately labeled Run artworks. This seasonal campaign source does not resolve the complete brand-book wishlist. [Offline collection](Nike-Sportswear/index.html) · [Source inventory and scope](Nike-Sportswear/manifests/README.md)

Opolis’s internal Nike Sportswear launch materials are documented as a 30-page Launch Book and a 70-page Eight Book. The design firm is credited and the entry shows selected artwork. Another documented lead is the 52-page AW77 Fleece Brand Book. Neither source supplied a complete page sequence during this search. [Communication Arts project credits](https://www.commarts.com/project/16746/nike-sportswear-brochure) · [AW77 production record](https://www.paperspecs.com/gallery/marketing-collateral/tnanike-sportswear-fleece-brand-book-3/)

Rosie Lee documents a UK relaunch book, and Doubleday & Cartwright documents the seasonal 4th & Inches publication and its historical Zinio distribution. Both are leads for obtaining originals. [Relaunch project](https://www.behance.net/gallery/236389/Nike-The-Relaunch-of-Nike-Sportswear) · [4th & Inches project](https://www.behance.net/gallery/3871433/Nike-Sportswear-presents-4th-Inches)
