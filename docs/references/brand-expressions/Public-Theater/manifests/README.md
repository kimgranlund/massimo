# The Public Theater

Complete distinct visual sequence from [Pentagram’s The Public Theater case study](https://www.pentagram.com/work/the-public-theater), retrieved 2026-11-05. This is a historical agency case study, not a complete brand-guidelines book. The narrative discusses the identity from 1994, the 2005 and 2008 revisions, and later applications; the displayed artwork also includes the 2012–13 season and later street posters. Do not treat all specimens as one current rule set.

## Coverage and source fidelity

All 37 project artworks are included in displayed order: one hero and 36 body images. No project video or animation is embedded in this source. Responsive renditions, metadata thumbnails and the unrelated Curious Pictures next-project teaser are excluded. The full source HTML and an inventory of exact signed download URLs and dimensions are retained in `../sources/`. The hero retains the publisher’s crop. Body sources retain their complete published artwork. PNGs preserve the decoded RGB pixels of their JPEG sources; no upscaling, reconstruction or re-typesetting was used. Published images range from 395×960 to 3000×2000; small original specimens limit fine-print legibility. Tiny dates, credits and color values are not invented.

Full-source prose is preserved in the HTML. Intent purpose evidence explicitly attributes the agency’s rationale; other strategic effects remain interpretations. The source contains historical examples rather than numerical usage specifications, so no Constraint purpose is assigned. Film, audio, unseen pages and a master manual are not claimed. Composite poster arrays remain single references because their comparisons and arrangement teach a distinct lesson; independently published posters are separate entries.
